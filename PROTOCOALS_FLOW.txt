  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

  ↓

GPIOC → AHB1 BUS
  ↓

  ↓

  ↓
Bit0 → GPIOA clock enable
Bit2 → GPIOC clock enable
  ↓
Set bit0 in RCC_AHB1ENR register
  ↓
GPIOA clock enabled
  ↓
Set bit2 in RCC_AHB1ENR register
  ↓
GPIOC clock enabled
  ↓
Open GPIO chapter in Reference Manual
  ↓
Look into MODER register
  ↓
Check how many bits control one pin
  ↓
Each pin uses 2 bits
  ↓
For PA5:
Use MODER bits 10 and 11
(2 × pin number)
  ↓
Look into MODER table
  ↓
00 → Input
01 → Output
10 → Alternate function
11 → Analog
  ↓
Clear MODER bits 10 and 11
  ↓
Write 01 into bits 10 and 11
  ↓
PA5 configured as OUTPUT
  ↓
For PC13:
Use MODER bits 26 and 27
  ↓
Clear bits 26 and 27
  ↓
Write 00 into bits 26 and 27
  ↓
PC13 configured as INPUT
  ↓
Look into IDR register
(IDR = Input Data Register)
  ↓
Check bit13 of IDR register
  ↓
Read button state from PC13
  ↓
Button pressed?
  ↓ YES
Look into ODR register
(ODR = Output Data Register)
  ↓
Set bit5 in ODR register
  ↓
LED ON
  ↓
Delay
  ↓
Clear bit5 in ODR register
  ↓
LED OFF
  ↓
Delay
  ↓
Go back and read button again
  ↓
Repeat forever

  ↓

  ↓ YES

  ↓
